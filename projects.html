COVID 19 Contact Tracing — Internet of COVID-19 Thiings

The Internet of COVID-19 Things (IoCT) workplace-reopening project integrated BLE proximity data with deep-learning camera/audio sensors in a hybrid edge-to-cloud system to assess room-level risk in real time. I served as the ML/CV developer and data scientist, training and deploying models to detect individual and group risky behaviors—including coughing, hugging, handshaking, and doorknob touching, as well as people density and physical distancing—from camera streams. Inference ran on an NVIDIA Jetson Xavier NX at the edge, with near-real-time ingestion and analytics in the cloud, and an interactive risk map for live and historical views—all aligned with OGC SensorThings and IndoorGML for interoperability.

Road Traffic management

I developed a Windows desktop Traffic Simulation GIS for intercity roads using C# (WPF/Windows Forms), ArcObjects, and the ArcGIS Engine SDK for Windows. The application performs equilibrium traffic assignment to redistribute vehicle flows and evaluate diversion strategies when road segments are blocked. Users can collect, query, and edit GIS layers to update conditions and estimate risk per road segment, enabling data-driven decisions during disruptions. I also designed the SQL Server relational database that stores the network, scenarios, and results.
